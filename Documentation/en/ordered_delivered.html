Ordered delivered

For every item with Delivered control it shows, day by day and for lunch and dinner, how much was ordered and not delivered yet and how much was delivered. It helps for example the kitchen or the grill to know how many portions to prepare.

Without the Order progress management of the Settings, everything goes to delivered at once.

  • Event, Event date and L/D (lunch or dinner): the turn.
  • Item description.
  • Ordered: the quantity in the orders whose department has not delivered yet.
  • Delivered: the quantity already delivered, that is read in Order progress.

Only Ordered > 0 hides the items with nothing ordered. The filter chooses event, date and day part; the print uses the same filter.

A kit counts as a whole kit; a menu is counted in its items.
