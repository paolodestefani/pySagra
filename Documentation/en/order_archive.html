Orders

The archive of the saved orders. It is mainly used to print again the copies of an order when a printer got stuck or the network had problems.

  • On opening, the filter chooses which orders to see; the list shows number, date, time, delivery, amounts, status and the other data of the header.
  • The data of the header, as table, covers, customer and status, can be corrected and saved.
  • Order details and Departments details show the lines of the order and how they were split among the departments; they are read only, because a change would not update the department copies and the inventory.
  • Department notes and status: the notes and the barcode of each department copy.

Reprint

In Current order reprint check the copies to print again, Customer, Cover and the departments, and press Print. The active copies depend on the Settings; an order with no covers has no cover copy.

New orders are created only in the Order entry window. Deleting an order deletes its lines too, and updates inventory and delivered quantities.
