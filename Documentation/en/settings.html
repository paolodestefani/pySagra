Settings

The settings of the current company, valid for all the computers.

Main

  • Formats: the decimals of quantities, prices and amounts, and the currency symbol.
  • New item normal stock level colors: the colors proposed for the buttons of salable items.
  • Items inventory stock threshold levels: below the warning level and below the critical one the stock of the inventory items changes color; the warning level always stays above the critical one.
  • Order progress management (ordered delivered): when on, the department copies have a barcode and the order becomes processed when the departments read it in Order progress; when off, every order is processed as soon as it is saved.

Order entry UI

The look of the order entry window: the layout, the Department tab position, rows, columns, spacing and font of the item grid and of the table grid, and the button colors for warning, critical and sold out stock.

Order entry control and print

  • Lunch / Dinner: the order day goes from 8:00 to 8:00 of the day after. The slider, or the field next to it, chooses the dinner start, between 9:00 and 23:00: before it is lunch, after it is dinner. The orders after midnight belong to the dinner of the day before.
  • Default delivery and Default payment: the values proposed for every new order.
  • Form parameters: table list, stock always shown, automatic variants, required table and name, max covers value; see the Order entry page.
  • Order number based on: Event, Day or Day part, that is when the numbers start again from 1; see Order numbers.
  • Order print copy: turns the customer copy, the department copies and the cover copy on or off for all the computers.
  • Menu description on menu items: on the department copies, the menu name appears next to the items of a menu.
  • Check inactivity after: after how many seconds with no orders the window proposes to refresh.

When saving fails, the window stays open with the changes.
