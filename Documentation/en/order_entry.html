Order entry

This is the cash desk window: here the customer order is made, the payment is taken and, on confirm, the order is saved and its copies are printed. It opens only when an event is running, that is an event that includes the date and time of now.

Delivery and customer

  • Deliver to: Table or Take-away; the proposed one is set in the Settings. For take-away only the tabs of the departments that prepare for takeaway are active.
  • Table number: typed, or, when the Use table list option of the Settings is on, chosen with a click on the seat map. The Settings can make it required; a table that is not in the map is reported.
  • Covers: the number of covers; above the Max covers value of the Settings a warning appears. For a table order with no covers the program asks whether to correct it.
  • Customer name and Customer contact: for take-away the name can be required, as set in the Settings.

Items

  • Every department has its tab with the buttons of the salable items; a click adds the item to the order.
  • Quantity 1, 5 or 10: how many pieces the next click adds; after the click it goes back to 1.
  • A click on an order line removes the chosen quantity, and the line disappears when it reaches zero.
  • Choose variants: for items with variants it opens the choice of variants, quantity and price. When the Show variants automatically option of the Settings is on, the choice always opens by itself.
  • Show stock: shows on the buttons the available quantity of the items with inventory control. The warning and critical colors say that the stock is running out; a sold out item can not be added. The availability takes the items already in the order into account.
  • Department notes: a message for a department, printed on its copy.

Payment

  • Amount, Discount and Total: the discount can not be greater than the amount.
  • Cash and Change: typing the cash received, the program computes the change.
  • Elect. Paym.: electronic payment; cash and change are cleared.
  • Confirm saves the order and prints the copies; Cancel empties the order. Before saving the program checks the stock and reports the items that are not enough, asking whether to go on.

Web orders

Web order input takes the QR code of an order made on the web site: Ctrl+F11 moves the cursor to the field, and the barcode reader fills the order with delivery, table, customer and items. The WO check says the order comes from the web. Items not available are reported and left out.

Other functions

  • Ctrl+F12: changes date and time, to enter orders of another moment or of another event; prices and stock are loaded again for the event of the chosen date.
  • When the inactivity check of the Settings is on, after the set time with no orders the program proposes to refresh the window, to have current prices and stock.
  • Esc closes the window, after a confirm.

The order gets a number as set by the Order number based on option of the Settings, and it belongs to lunch or dinner by its time: lunch goes from 8:00 to the dinner start, dinner until 8:00 of the day after.
