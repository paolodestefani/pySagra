Price lists

A price list is a list of item prices. There can be several price lists, for example one for the summer festival and one for the autumn one: every event uses the price list chosen in the Events form, and the order entry window uses the prices of that list.

  • Price list description: the name of the price list, required.
  • Price list prices: the items and their price. An item can appear only once in each price list. The prices can be seen and changed in the Prices tab of the Items too.

A salable item with no price in the price list of the event appears in the order entry window with price zero.

Duplicating a price list

Duplicate creates a new price list with all the prices of the current one: it is the quickest way to prepare the price list of a new event and then change only the prices that need it.

Deleting

Before deleting a price list the program lists the events that use it: after the delete they have no price list, and in the order entry window their prices become zero.
