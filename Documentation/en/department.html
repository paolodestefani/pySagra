Departments

Departments are the parts of the organization that prepare and deliver the items: kitchen, grill, bar, pizzeria and so on. Every item belongs to a department.

  • Department description: the name of the department. It can not be the same as the one of another department. It is the name of the tab in the order entry window and it is printed on the department copy.
  • Sorting: the order of the departments, for the tabs of the order entry window and for the prints.
  • Menu: the department holds the menus. It has its own tab in the order entry window, but no department copy: when the order is saved, every menu is split into its items, which go to the copies of their departments.
  • Takeaway: the department prepares for takeaway too. In a takeaway order only the tabs of the departments with this check are active.
  • Obsolete: the department is no longer used and does not appear in the order entry window and in the lists.

For every department, the Order print settings form sets on which printer and with which report the department copy of each computer is printed.

Deleting

A department can not be deleted when it has items or when it is used in orders: the program shows a message. In these cases check Obsolete instead.
