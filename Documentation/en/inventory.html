Inventory

The stock of the items with Inventory control, event by event. On opening it works on the running event; the filter chooses another one.

Normal items inventory

  • Item: add the rows of the items to control; an item can appear only once for each event.
  • Load: the loaded quantity, for example the pieces that arrived.
  • Unload: the delivered quantity, that is read in Order progress.
  • Stock: load minus unload, what is still there.
  • Ordered: the quantity in the orders not delivered yet. Without the Order progress management of the Settings it stays at zero, because everything goes to unload at once.
  • Available: stock minus ordered, what can still be sold. It is the value shown on the buttons of the order entry window.
  • New stock: for a count, type the counted stock and the program corrects the load.

The stock and available columns take the warning and critical colors by the levels of the Settings. On save the inventory is computed again from the orders of the event, so an item added after it was already sold starts with the right values.

Kit and menu availability

Kits and menus have no stock of their own: the Kit availability and Menu availability tables show how many of them can still be sold, computed from the components with inventory control.
