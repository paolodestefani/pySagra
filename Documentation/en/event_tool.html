Event based tool

Maintenance operations on the data of an event. Choose the Event and the operation: the Warning box says what it does, and the program asks for a confirm before running it.

  • Delete Orders: deletes all the orders of the event, with ordered/delivered and numbering, and clears unload and ordered of the inventory. It can not be undone: it is used for example to remove the test orders before opening.
  • Inventory rebuild: computes unload and ordered of the inventory again from the orders. It is needed after changing the controls of the items or the components of a kit.
  • Ordered delivered rebuild: computes ordered and delivered again from the orders.
  • Numbering Rebuild: computes the order counters again from the saved orders.
  • Mark Orders as Processed: marks as processed, at the order date, all the orders not processed yet; it is used for example at the end of the event, when the order progress was not used.
